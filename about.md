# Sorrow Integration

by **endrosity**

Sorrow Integration connects Geometry Dash to the **Sorrow Discord bot**. Your Glimmer, bets, roulette games and challenges sync between the game and the bot.

## Glimmer & Betting

- Earn **Glimmer**, the currency you share with the Sorrow bot, and watch your balance in game.
- **Bet Glimmer** on reaching a percent in a level. Payouts depend on the level's difficulty.
- You can place one bet per attempt. A badge in the top left shows when a bet is active.
- Progress bar effects build up as you get closer to the percent you bet on.
- Win and loss animations play on your balance, and the full amount counts up or down.

## Roulette

- A redesigned **Home screen** with **Solo** and **Multiplayer** modes.
- **Solo:** choose difficulties in a new picker with every difficulty icon, from Easy to Extreme Demon.
- **Multiplayer rooms:** create or join a room and play the same random levels together.
  - Modes: **Classic**, **Teams** and **Elimination**.
  - Bet on the room before the host starts it.
  - **Room history** shows past games and results.
- Each level's song loads correctly, and the goal percent shows on the progress bar.

## Versus Duels

- GD's **Versus** button on the Create page works now. It opens the duels screen.
- **Challenge** a player 1v1, or open a **Race** for up to 8 players. Winner takes the Glimmer pot.
- **Quick Match:** Ranked or Casual matchmaking that pairs you with someone near your Elo.
- Four modes: **Race** (first to the target %, with completion times), **Showdown** (highest % before time runs out), **Consistency** (every attempt counts, highest average %) and **Sudden Death** (one attempt each), best of 1, 3 or 5.
- **Tournaments:** single-elimination cups for 4 or 8 players with an entry fee. 1st place gets 70% of the prize pool and 2nd gets 30%.
- **Seasons:** every month ends with Glimmer rewards for your final rank, plus bonuses for the top 3.
- **Ranked** duels move your Elo, from Bronze up to Champion. You get rank trophies, rank-up celebrations, a leaderboard and duel history.
- Everyone is taken to each round's level and starts on a 3-2-1-GO countdown. An in-level panel shows the score, everyone's % and the clock.
- Spectators can **side-bet** on who wins. Rematch with one tap.
- **Globed:** players near you show up to challenge, and during a duel you only see your opponents.
- Duel on a specific level with the **VS** button on any level page, or from Discord with /gd duel.

## Daily & Weekly Challenges

- **Daily and weekly challenges** ask you to beat a specific rated level or reach a percent on a demon, with big Glimmer rewards.
- Levels you've already beaten or passed the target on are never picked.

## Globed Support

- Players with the mod show **WIN** or **LOSS** under their icon on the progress bar when their bet resolves.
- While you're in a roulette room, you only see the players in your room.

## Setup

Link your account in the mod settings using the details the Sorrow bot gives you. Then your Glimmer and games sync automatically.
